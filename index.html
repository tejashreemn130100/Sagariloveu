Hey Kandamma, ❤️

Nimge gottho gothilvo… nange neevu andre praana. Munche yaaradru nange yaaru tumba ishta andre, first nanna appa antha helthidde. Adre eega nanna life alli neevu eshtu important andre, nange yellariginta, yella vishayakkinta munche neevu.

Nijavaglu nanu ishtondu change aagthini antha ankondiralilla. Nanna friends yella kelthare, “Neenu daily ishtondu kashta pattu travel maadthidiya, ninage sustha aagalla?” antha. Nange nijavaglu ashtondu kashta ansoodilla. Oppkothini, kelavu dina “Saaku, ee travel saaku” anisutte. Adre matte nanna manassu helutte, “Beda, nanna Kandana nodbeku, avara jothe irbeku andre innondhu dina travel maadidru parvagilla” antha.

Kanda, neevu nange enu jadhu maadidiro gothilla ri. 😘 Neevu nanage toriso preethi, care, aa ondu kaalaji ideyalla… adu nange tumba vishayagalanna kalistha ide. Patience andre enu, obbarigagi adjust aagodu andre enu, namma preethisoru namma life alli idre naavu eshtu strong aagi irbahudu annodanna nimage gottilladene nanage kalisthidira.

Nimanna first time nodidaga, nijavaglu neevu nanage perfect alla antha anisittu. Swalpa dina nan madida decision correct aa antha kooda yochane maadidde. Adre nimma bagge jaasthi tilkoloke shuru maadidaga, neevu eshtu olleya manushya, family bagge eshtu kaalaji iruva vyakthi, nanna bagge eshtu preethi ittkondiddira annodu artha aaythu.

Naanu modalu nodiddu nimma horagina vyakthitvavanna. Adre dina kaledante nimma manassanna artha maadkoloke shuru maadide. Eega nanage anisodu, nanna life alli nanage sigabekada obba olleya manushyana devaru nanage kottiddare antha. ❤️

Howdu kanda, avattu nanage nijavaglu tumba koppa bandittu. “En guru, naanu eno ankondhu maduve aade, adre illi ella reverse aagtha ideyalla! Ee huduganige horage hogbeku antha anisalwa?” antha ankondidde. Adakke avattu nim jothe swalpa loose thara maathadidde. 😅 Adre nija helbekandre, aa koppa hinde kooda nim jothe irbeku, nim jothe time spend maadbeku annuva aase ittu.

Actually, nanagu nanna maduve aada hudugana jothe road road suthbeku, hosa hosa places nodbeku, night walks hogbeku, summane kai hidkondhu maathadbeku, kelavomme enu maathadade pakkadalli kootkondhu time spend maadbeku antha tumba aase ittu.

Nim jothe late morning alli nidde maadodu, yaaru disturb maadade ibbaru namma time enjoy maadodu, sudden aagi long drive hogodu, horage hogi enadru tindu, sustha aagovargu suthi, manege bandu nemmadiaagi malagodu… ivella nanna chikka chikka aasegalu kanda. ❤️

Adakke naanu mane maadona antha heliddu. Life alli pratiyondu vishayakku ondu phase irutte alva? Makkalu aada mele responsibility jaasthi aagutte. Aaga namma ibbara time, namma me-time, ee thara sudden plans maadoke sigova avakasha kadime aagbahudu. Adakke maduve aada hosadaralli, namma life na naavu ibbaru chennagi enjoy maadbeku antha aase pattidde.

Aamele nanage artha aaythu, maduve aada takshana bere hogbeku, suthbeku antha yella couples ankondru adakke bere bere kaaranagalu irutte. Kelavomme kelasa, responsibility, duddu, susthu… ivella namma aasegalanna swalpa hindakke haakbidutte.

Adre kanda, nanage ondu vishaya maatra tumba important. Namma life hegirali, naavu elli irali, namma hatra eshtu duddu irali annodakkinta, naavu ibbaru eshtu khushiyaagi iddivi annodu nanage mukhya. Namma hatra dodda mane irali, dodda car irali, tumba duddu irali annodakkinta, aa maneyalli naanu nim jothe nagutha irbeku. Aa car alli naavu ibbaru jothege travel maadbeku. Aa duddu inda naavu namma kanasugalanna nanasu maadkolbeku.

Nanna life alli nange bekaagirodu neevu kanda. Nim jothe ondu sundaravaada jeevana kattikollodu. Namma kashtagalalli obbarigobbaru dhairya aagodu. Namma santoshagalanna jothege celebrate maadodu. Namma jagala mugida mele matte obbaranna obbaru hudukikondu barodu. Yaaru sari, yaaru tappu annodakkinta, namma relationship sari irbeku antha ibbaru yochane maadodu.

Kanda, naanu nimma mele tumba possessive. Nange nim andre tumba ishta. Naanu obbaranna nanna manassinda preethisoke shuru maadidre, avarige koduva importance bere yaarigu kodalla. Avaru nanage eshtu special annodanna avarige torisbeku anisutte. Neevu nanage antaha special person.

Nanna jeevanadalli bere yaaru eshte important aagidru, nanna husband aagi, nanna partner aagi, nanna life na share maadkolluva vyakthiyaagi nimma sthana yaavaglu bere. Naanu nimma kadege thirugi nododu yaake andre, nanna manassige nemmadi sigodu nimma hatra.

Kelavomme naanu jaasthi yochane maadbahudu, jaasthi expect maadbahudu, chikka vishayakku koppa maadkollabahudu. Adre aa ella feelings hinde iruvudu onde ondu kaarana — naanu nimmanna tumba preethisthini. Nimma jothe iruva sambandha nanage tumba amoolya. Adanna naanu endigu kaledukolloke ishtapadalla.

Naanu perfect alla kanda, neevu kooda perfect alla. Adre naavu ibbaru obbarigobbaru perfect partner aagoke prayathna maadbahudu alva? Naanu nimma jothe beleybeku, nimma kanasugalige support maadbeku, neevu susthaagi manege bandaga nimma nemmadiaagbeku. Nimma santoshadalli nanna santosha irbeku. Neevu kashtadalli iddaga, “Naaniddini kanda, chinthe maadbedi” antha heluva vyakthi naanaagbeku.

Innondu vishaya helbeku kanda… nanna life alli naanu tumba kanasu kandiddini. Career, family, travel, mane, makkalu… heege tumba. Adre eega aa ella kanasugalanna nimma jothe nanasu maadbeku anisutte. Namma putta family, namma magu, naavu ibbaru seri maguvige koduva preethi, namma chikka chikka memories… ivellavannoo nenesikondre manassige tumba khushi aagutte.

Namma life ge ondu dina namma magu bandaga, neevu aa maguvanna preethiyinda ettikolluvudanna nodbeku antha nanage tumba aase ide. Namma preethi innondu roopadalli namma munde iruva aa dina barali antha manasaare bayasthini. Adhu yaavaga aagali, aa payanadalli naavu ibbaru obbarigobbaru support aagbeku. ❤️

Nimge ondu vishaya gottirbeku kanda… naanu nimma jothe iruvaga yaavaglu khushiyaagirbeku antha illa. Kelavomme althini, koppa maadkothini, jagala maadthini. Adre aa ella samayadallu nanna manassu bayasodu nimma jothe maatadi, matte sari hogbeku annodanne. Nimminda doora hogbeku annodalla; nimma jothe innu hattiravaagbeku annodu.

Nanna dina eshte kashtavaagidru, nimma jothe swalpa maathadidre saaku, manassige nemmadi sigutte. Naanu eshte susthaagi bandru, nimma mukha nodidaga aa susthu kadime aagutte. Neevu nanna hatra iddira annuva ondu bhaavane nanage dhairya kodutte.

Nanna Kandamma, nanna life ge banda mele nanna jeevana ishtondu badalaagutte antha naanu ankondiralilla. Naanu yaarigagi ishtondu care maadthini, yaarigagi ishtondu yochane maadthini, yaarigagi nanna aasegalanna badalaayisikolloke ready aagthini antha nanage gottiralilla. Adre eega gothaagide — preethi andre bari “I love you” helodu alla. Obbara bagge yochane maadodu, avara susthanna artha maadkolodu, avara kashtagalalli jotheyaagodu, avara jothe jeevanavanne kaleybeku antha aase padodu.

Ultimately, naanu nimge enu helbeku andre… nanage neevu andre tumba ishta kanda. Tumba andre tumba. ❤️

Nanna preethige dodda dodda kaaranagalu bekilla. Neevu nanna jothe iruvudu, nanna bagge yochane maadodu, nanna kaalaji vahisodu, nanna jothe nimma jeevanavanna share maadkollodu… ivella saaku.

Naanu nimma mele eshtu preethi ittkondiddini annodanna kelavomme sariyagi heloke nanage barade irabahudu. Adre nanna manassalli nimma sthana eshtu doddadu annodanna maatra yaavattu kadime maadkollalla.

Nanna jeevanadalli eshte jana bandru, eshte badalaavanegalu aadru, nanna kai hidkondhu nanna jothe ee jeevanavanna nadeyabekada vyakthi neevu. Namma vayassu hecchaadru, namma mukha mele wrinkles bandru, naavu modlina thara odadoke aagade hodru, naanu nimma kai hidkondhu nimma pakkadalli koothkondhu, “Kanda, nenapideya naavu hege suththadthidvi?” antha nagbeku.

Aaga kooda neevu nanna nodi nagbeku. Naanu kooda nimma nodi, “Nimma mele nanage ivattigu ashte preethi ide” antha helbeku.

Nanna jeevanadalli naanu padeyuva dodda gift enu gotha? Nanna jeevanada ella phases nalloo nimma jothe iruva avakasha. Nimma hendathiyaagi, nimma friend aagi, nimma strength aagi, nimma favourite person aagi irbeku annodu.

Aste kanda… nanage neevu andre jasthi ishta. Jasthi preethi. Jasthi kaalaji. Jasthi aasegalu. Ella kinta jaasthi neevu beku. ❤️

Nimma jothe jagala maadbeku, nimma jothe nagbeku, nimma jothe jagattu suthbeku, nimma jothe mane kattbeku, nimma jothe namma makkala belavanige nodbeku, nimma jothe vayassaagbeku. Innu eshto janmagalu iddru, nanna jothe heege iruva vyakthi neevagiye irbeku antha aase.

Nanna Kandamma, neevu nanage husband maatra alla. Neevu nanna friend, nanna comfort, nanna favourite person, nanna future, nanna family.

I love you so much, Kandamma. Nanna jeevanadalli banda nanna favourite person neevu. Nanna Sagar, nanna preethi, nanna nemmadi. ❤️😘

Yaavaglu heege nanna kai hidkondhu iri. Naavu ibbaru seri namma jeevanavanna sundaravaagi kattikollona. Yella kanasugalanna ondondagi nanasu maadona. Kashta bandru ottige edurisoNa, santosha bandru ottige enjoy maadona.
